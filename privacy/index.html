Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026.

1. Data Controller

The controller of your data is the solo founder and independent developer behind the Eclipse Client project, operating as an individual project with no external corporate team. For data protection inquiries or requests, contact the Founder through our official Discord support server.

2. Website & Analytics

  • Google Analytics: Our website uses Google Analytics 4 to collect usage information such as page views, interactions, general geographic region, and device type to help us understand traffic and improve the site. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar identifiers. We do not use this data for advertising or sell it to advertisers.
  • Server logs: Our hosting provider may briefly process technical logs such as IP address, timestamp, requested pages, and browser information to maintain site security, prevent abuse, and ensure network stability. These logs are not used for user profiling.

3. Launcher & Microsoft Authentication

  • Password access: Microsoft sign-in takes place through Microsoft’s official authentication interface. Eclipse Client does not see, intercept, or store your Microsoft password.
  • Local token storage: OAuth access tokens are encrypted locally on your computer using operating-system security stores, such as DPAPI on Windows or Keychain on macOS.
  • No central token server: Authentication credentials and access tokens stay on your device and are not transmitted to or stored on Eclipse servers.

4. Client Features & Technical Data

  • Account identification: To connect to Minecraft services, the launcher processes your Minecraft account UUID and in-game name locally.
  • Network information: Your IP address is processed during web requests or server connections for technical purposes such as rate limiting and connection management. It is not attached to a permanent tracking profile by Eclipse.

5. Data Sharing & Infrastructure

  • No data selling: We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information or analytics data to third-party advertisers or data brokers.
  • Service providers: Website and launcher distribution files are hosted on third-party platforms, including GitHub Pages and content delivery networks, which process technical data to deliver and secure the service.

6. Your Rights

Depending on the data protection laws that apply to you, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal data associated with your interactions with our services. To make a request, contact us through our official Discord community support channel.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes to our software, services, or legal requirements. The latest version will be published on this page.
