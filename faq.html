General & Safety

1. What is Eclipse Launcher?

Eclipse is a lightweight, custom Minecraft: Java Edition launcher and client modpack designed for speed, clean customization, and hassle-free mod management—built to get you into your game faster without bloated menus.

2. Is Eclipse Launcher safe and free to use?

Yes, Eclipse is 100% free to use. Your account security is a top priority—we never ask for or store your Microsoft password; all official login authentication is handled securely through Microsoft OAuth.

3. Is this a hacked client?

No, not at all! Eclipse is a legitimate, standard client for Minecraft: Java Edition—just like other popular PvP and performance clients. It only includes client-side performance boosts and visual quality-of-life features, with zero unfair advantages or cheats. As always, check specific server rules if you decide to add extra third-party mods on your own.

4. What about other “Eclipse” modpacks on Modrinth, GitHub launchers, or Bedrock hacked clients?

Those are completely separate, abandoned projects that nobody uses anymore, and they have zero affiliation with us. Make sure you are only downloading from our official website: https://eclipse-launcher.github.io/

5. Who created Eclipse Client?

Eclipse is created and maintained by Omar Hasan Arman, a solo developer and student who loves building cool, fast, and customizable software from scratch.

Compatibility & Setup

6. Can I play with an offline account?

Yes, offline accounts are supported! However, keep in mind that playing offline comes with limitations—such as custom skins not loading properly and not being able to join online-mode premium servers. For the full experience, you can easily sign in using your official Microsoft account.

7. Which Minecraft versions, mod loaders, and operating systems are supported?

Eclipse runs on Windows (64-bit) and gives you full freedom over how you play. It works both as a standalone launcher supporting all major versions and mod loaders (Fabric, Forge, Vanilla, etc.) and as an optional, fully customizable Client Modpack—the choice is yours.

8. Can I add my own mods, shaders, or resource packs?

Absolutely. You can drop your own compatible .jar mods, shaders, and resource packs directly into your instance folder without breaking any of the built-in client features.

Troubleshooting & Support

9. Windows SmartScreen showed a warning when I ran the installer. Why?

Because Eclipse is an independent project built by a solo developer without a costly corporate code-signing certificate, Windows may show an “Unknown Publisher” prompt on new releases. Simply click More info → Run anyway to proceed safely.

10. My game is lagging or won’t launch. How do I fix it?

First, check your RAM allocation in Eclipse’s settings (4 GB is recommended for smooth gameplay—make sure you don’t allocate all of your PC’s memory!). Also ensure you have the proper Java runtime installed and your graphics drivers are up to date.

11. Where can I report a bug or suggest a new feature?

Join our official Discord server using the button at the top of the page! Drop your bug reports, crash logs, or feature ideas in the support channels and we’ll look into them.
